# Car Service Management System

**Car Service Management System** is a multi-tenant web application designed for automotive service companies to manage their daily operations in one centralized platform.

The system allows multiple automotive service companies to use the same application while keeping their customers, vehicles, employees, work orders, inventory, and financial data completely separated.

## Main Features
✅ Multi-tenant architecture with isolated company data
✅ User authentication and role-based authorization
✅ Company and employee management
✅ Customer and vehicle management
✅ Appointment scheduling
✅ Work order management and repair tracking
✅ Vehicle inspection and service history
✅ Service and spare parts management
✅ Inventory and supplier management
✅ Invoice and payment management
✅ Work order status history
✅ Notifications and service updates
✅ Company-specific business settings and pricing
✅ Dashboard with operational and financial statistics
✅ Customer service history and vehicle records
